⚽ AC FC | In-Game Rules

Please follow these rules during all official AC FC matches. Failure to do so may result in penalties or even kicked out the game.

1. Goalkeeper Line
Players may not cross the goalkeeper line. Doing so will result in a penalty kick to the opposing team.
2. Kickoff
Do not kick the ball before the countdown. Early kicks will result in a free kick for the opposing team.
3. Chat Conduct
Cursing is allowed however, slurs will get you blacklisted or even the <@&1520534447886438521> for a couple of days.
4. Breaks
Players may not take repeated breaks during a match.
5. Goal Box
Only the goalkeeper is ALOWED to touch the ball inside the goal box. Even if its your own teammate
6. Roster Changes
If a new player joins your team after a match that your team won that player is not able to participate in the following matches.
7. Minimum Players
Each team must have at least four (4) players in game to begin a match Teams that fail to meet the requirement will be disqualified.
📌 Notice
These rules are able to change at any time.
